    Terms of use

    Draft for review. Have a legal advisor check this text before you rely on it.

    1. Who can use it

    You must be 18 or older and sign in with your own Google account. One person, one account.

    2. Fair play

    No cheating, hacks, fake accounts or abuse of other players. Accounts that break these rules can be removed and their entries cancelled.

    3. Coins and entry

    Coins are used to join matches. Entry fees are shown on each match and are deducted when you join. Room details are shared only with players who joined.

    4. Results

    Match results and rankings are published by the organizers and are final unless a clear mistake is found.

    5. Changes

    Matches, prizes and these terms can change. Continuing to use the platform means you accept the current version.

    NST is the organizer. Contact details will be added here.

    Privacy policy

    Draft for review. Have a legal advisor check this text before you rely on it.

    What we store

    From your Google account: name, email address and profile photo. From you: your 18+ confirmation, in-game name and Free Fire UID. From your activity: the matches you join and your coin balance.

    Why

    To sign you in, let you join matches, show your room details and results, and run rankings.

    Who can see it

    Your profile and wallet are readable only by you. Room details are readable only by players who joined that match. Rankings and results show names only.

    Where it is kept

    Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore).

    Your choices

    You can log out at any time. To ask for your data to be corrected or removed, contact NST. Contact details will be added here.
